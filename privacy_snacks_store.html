Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Snack Store (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use our mobile game application.

Snack Store is a family-friendly puzzle game designed for all ages, including children. We are fully committed to protecting user privacy and providing a safe gaming environment.

1. Information We Collect

Snack Store does not collect any personal information.

We do NOT collect, store, or request:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical or precise location data
  • Photos, videos, or media files
  • Voice recordings
  • Contacts
  • Personally identifiable device information

All game progress (such as completed levels and move counts) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Snack Store is suitable for children and is designed to comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

We do not knowingly collect or request any personal data from children. Parents and guardians can feel confident allowing children to play Snack Store without sharing any personal information.

3. Advertisements

Snack Store displays limited, family-safe advertisements.

These ads are shown only to support the development and maintenance of the game.

Important points regarding ads:

  • Ads do not collect personal information
  • Ads are non-personalized
  • No behavioral tracking or interest-based profiling is performed

All advertisements shown comply with Google Play’s Families Policy.

4. Analytics & Tracking

We do not use analytics tools, profiling systems, or tracking technologies that identify individual users.

5. In-App Purchases

Snack Store does not include any in-app purchases or hidden costs.

6. Internet Usage

While the game can be played offline, an internet connection may be used solely to load advertisements. There are no social media integrations or chat features within the app.

7. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or stored on our servers, there is no risk of personal data leakage or misuse.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
