Privacy Policy

Welcome to ParkFlow. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our players. This Privacy Policy explains how ParkFlow ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you play our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

When you play ParkFlow, we may collect certain information automatically, including:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (like Advertising ID), and language settings.
  • Usage Data: Information about your gameplay, such as levels completed, scores, in-game progress, and time spent in the app.
  • Technical Logs: IP addresses and crash reports to help us improve game stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the game experience.
  • To track your progress and high scores in the Parking Jam puzzles.
  • To analyze gameplay trends and fix bugs/crashes.
  • To serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements (if applicable).

3. Third-Party Services

ParkFlow may use third-party tools that collect data to help us run the game. These may include:

  • Analytics: (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase, Unity Analytics) to understand how players interact with the game.
  • Advertising: (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads) to show ads that keep the game free to play.
  • Cloud Saves: (e.g., Google Play Games, Apple Game Center) to sync your progress across devices.

4. Children's Privacy

ParkFlow is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide you with the game services. You may request the deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email address provided below.

6. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the correction or deletion of your data.
  • Opt-out of targeted advertising through your device settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the latest date.
