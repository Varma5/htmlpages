Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Fish Sort Saga (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use our mobile game application.

Fish Sort Saga is a family-friendly puzzle game designed for children. We are fully committed to protecting user privacy, especially the privacy of children.

1. Information We Collect

Fish Sort Saga does not collect any personal information.

We do NOT collect, store, or request:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Physical or precise location data
  • Photos, videos, or media files
  • Voice recordings
  • Contacts
  • Personally identifiable device information

All game progress (such as completed levels) is stored locally on the user’s device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Fish Sort Saga is suitable for children under the age of 13 and is designed to comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

We do not knowingly collect, request, or store any personal data from children. Parents and guardians can safely allow children to play this game without sharing any personal information.

3. Advertisements

Fish Sort Saga displays limited, family-safe advertisements.

These ads are shown only to support the development and maintenance of the game and to generate revenue.

Important points regarding ads:

  • Ads do not collect personal information
  • Ads are non-personalized
  • No behavioral tracking is performed
  • No interest-based or targeted advertising is used

All advertisements shown comply with Google Play’s Families Policy and are appropriate for children.

4. Analytics & Tracking

We do not use analytics tools, profiling systems, or tracking technologies.

User activity is not monitored, logged, or analyzed.

5. In-App Purchases

Fish Sort Saga does not include any in-app purchases.

6. Internet Usage & External Links

The game does not require an active internet connection for gameplay.

There are no external links, social media integrations, or chat features within the app.

7. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected, stored, or shared, there is no risk of personal data misuse or leakage.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
