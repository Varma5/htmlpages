Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application PuzzleNest (referred to as “the App”), developed and published by us.

Information Collection and Use

PuzzleNest does not collect any personal information.

We do not collect, store, or request access to:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Physical or precise location data
  • Photos, videos, or media files
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Voice recordings or messages
  • Personally identifiable information

Any game progress such as levels completed, scores, or settings is stored locally on the user's device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Children’s Privacy

PuzzleNest is designed for all age groups, including children under 13.

We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The App does not knowingly collect, request, or store personal data from children.

Advertisements

The App may display advertisements to support development and maintenance.

  • Ads do not collect personal information
  • Ads shown are non-personalized
  • No behavioral or interest-based tracking is used
  • Ads comply with Google Play policies, including Families Policy (if applicable)

Analytics and Tracking

We do not use analytics tools, tracking systems, or profiling technologies. User activity is not monitored or logged.

In-App Purchases

PuzzleNest does not contain any in-app purchases.

Internet Usage and External Links

The App does not require an active internet connection to play and does not include:

  • External website links
  • Social media integrations
  • Chat or messaging features

Data Security

Since no personal data is collected, stored, or shared, there is no risk of personal data misuse or unauthorized access.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
